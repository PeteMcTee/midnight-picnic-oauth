Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

This policy covers Midnight Picnic Bot, a Google OAuth client used to upload videos to YouTube channels that the operator already owns or administers.

What we access

With your Google Account consent, the bot may use YouTube APIs to upload and manage videos on those channels. It does not request access to unrelated Google products.

How tokens and data are stored

OAuth tokens and related credentials are stored on a private NAS / vault controlled by the operator. They are not placed in this public repository and are not shared with third parties for advertising or analytics.

We do not sell data

Midnight Picnic does not sell, rent, or trade personal information, YouTube content, or OAuth tokens.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app at any time in your Google Account (Google Account → Security → Third-party access). After revoke, stored tokens should be deleted from the operator’s vault.
